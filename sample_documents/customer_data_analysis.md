### Recommendations

1. **Targeted Marketing Campaigns**
   - Develop personalized email campaigns for the Premium Subscribers segment
   - Implement retargeting ads for cart abandoners
   - Create special offers for customers who also shop with CompanyX

2. **Product Development**
   - Focus on expanding product lines that appeal to the 25-34 age demographic
   - Develop premium options for high-value customers
   - Create entry-level products to convert more New Users to Frequent Shoppers

3. **Customer Experience Improvements**
   - Optimize mobile checkout process to reduce abandonment
   - Implement live chat support during peak shopping hours (7-9 PM EST)
   - Develop a loyalty program targeting Frequent Shoppers

### Conclusion
This analysis provides valuable insights into our customer base and their behaviors. By implementing the recommended strategies, we can increase customer retention, boost average order value, and effectively compete with our main rivals in the market.
