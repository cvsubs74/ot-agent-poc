### Risk Factors

1. **Macroeconomic Uncertainties**
   - Inflation pressures may impact margins in H2 2025
   - Interest rate fluctuations could affect financing costs
   - Currency exchange volatility in international markets

2. **Competitive Landscape**
   - New market entrant in Enterprise Solutions space
   - Pricing pressure in Consumer Products segment
   - Accelerated innovation from key competitors

3. **Supply Chain**
   - Continued component shortages for certain product lines
   - Logistics costs remain elevated compared to historical norms
   - Supplier concentration risks in Asia-Pacific region

4. **Regulatory Environment**
   - Pending data privacy legislation in key markets
   - Tax policy changes in European operations
   - Increased scrutiny of technology sector by regulators

### Conclusion
Our strong Q1 2025 performance demonstrates the effectiveness of our strategic initiatives and positions us well to exceed our full-year targets. The executive team should focus on maintaining momentum in high-growth areas while addressing potential headwinds in the competitive and regulatory landscape.
